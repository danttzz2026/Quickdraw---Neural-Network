How it works — model architecture

A small CNN with ~500K parameters. Each box is the shape of the feature map at that layer: the box gets wider as the number of channels grows, and shorter as the spatial resolution shrinks through max-pooling. Your 28×28 drawing enters at the left and is progressively transformed into a 15-class probability vector on the right.

Total parameters
~500,000 trainable weights
Conv 3×3
Slides a 3×3 filter across the image to detect local patterns (edges, curves, corners). Each layer learns dozens of different filters.
MaxPool 2×2
Keeps the strongest signal in each 2×2 block, halving the spatial size and making the representation translation-tolerant.
Flatten + Dense
Unrolls the 7×7×64 feature grid into a single vector of 3,136 numbers, then combines them into a 15-way classification.
Softmax
Squashes the 15 raw scores into probabilities that sum to 100% — what you see in the bars above.
